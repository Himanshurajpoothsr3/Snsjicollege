Our History

A Legacy of Learning in the Heart of Nature

S.N.S. Janta Intermediate College has served as a beacon of knowledge for the community since 2016. Our campus blends traditional values with modern educational methods in a naturally serene atmosphere.

We believe in fostering an environment where students can grow academically, socially, and morally. The college premises are blessed with abundant greenery, providing a peaceful atmosphere away from city noise.
